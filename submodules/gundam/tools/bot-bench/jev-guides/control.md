# Control

Control wins the long game. It survives the early turns with Blockers, Bases and removal, trades
one card for two where it can, and wins with a few powerful late-game Units once the opponent is
out of threats. Its Shields are a resource to spend, not something to protect at all costs.

## How to think
- Early on, the aim is not to lose too much, not to deal damage. Use Blockers, Bases and removal
  on the Units that actually threaten lethal.
- Card advantage is the win condition: answers that remove two things, Units that draw, Bases that
  return Shields to hand. Avoid trading a card for a small Unit when a Blocker can hold it.
- Save the best removal for the threats that matter: Link Units, <High-Maneuver>, <Suppression>,
  and anything that would win the game if left alone.
- Losing a Shield or two early is fine. Losing the board at 2 Shields is not. Tighten up as
  Shields drop.
- Switch from defence to offence once the opponent's hand and board are small and ours is
  stronger. Then close quickly; a long finish gives them draws to recover.
- Keep resources open for 【Action】 cards on the opponent's turn when they are the best play.

## Common mistakes
- Attacking early and leaving no Blockers, then losing to a fast deck's swing back.
- Using premium removal on a cheap Unit.
- Waiting too long to turn the corner, giving the opponent time to rebuild.

## Game plans

### survive_early
When: turns 1-4, or while the opponent has more Units than we do. Do: deploy Blockers and Bases,
keep Units active to block, remove attackers that would break two or more Shields. Only attack when
the attacking Unit is not needed on defence and survives the swing back.

### answer_threats
When: the opponent has a Link Unit, a Unit with <High-Maneuver> or <Suppression>, or a Unit we
cannot block profitably. Do: use removal on that Unit first. Prefer answers that also leave a Unit
or card behind. Block with Units that survive.

### take_over
When: we have our late-game Units on board and the opponent has 2 or fewer Units, or fewer cards
in hand than us. Do: attack with our strongest Units every turn while keeping one Blocker back.
Trade off anything new they deploy.

### close_the_game
When: the opponent has 2 or fewer Shields and no Base, or this turn's attacks can remove every
Shield and then hit them. Do: send everything needed, remove Blockers first, ignore card advantage.

### last_stand
When: we have 2 or fewer Shields and the opponent has more total AP than we can block. Do: block
with everything, use every removal and 【Action】 card on their attackers, deploy Bases and Blockers.
Survive this turn first.
