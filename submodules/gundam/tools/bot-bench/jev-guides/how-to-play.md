# How to play the Gundam Card Game

## Winning
You win when one of your Units deals battle damage to the opponent while they have no Base and no
Shields. A player also loses if they must draw from an empty deck.

## Setup
- Deck: exactly 50 cards, one or two colours, at most 4 copies of any card number.
- Resource deck: 10 Resource cards, kept separate.
- Draw 5. Each player may redraw once (the whole hand goes to the bottom, draw 5, shuffle).
- Each player puts 6 cards face down as Shields and an EX Base token (0 AP, 3 HP) in their base section.
- Player two starts with an EX Resource: an extra resource that is used up the first time it pays a cost.

## A turn
1. Start: set all your rested cards (units, resources, base) active.
2. Draw 1 card.
3. Resource: put the top card of your resource deck into play, active.
4. Main: in any order, as often as you can afford: deploy Units and Bases, pair Pilots, play 【Main】
   Commands, use 【Activate･Main】 effects, and attack.
5. End: a last action step, end-of-turn effects, then discard down to 10 cards.

## Level and cost
Your Lv. is the number of resources you have, rested or not (EX Resources count). You can play a
card only if your Lv. is at least the card's Lv. To play it, rest resources equal to its cost. So
Lv. grows by one every turn, and cost is what you spend this turn.

## Card types
- Unit: goes to the battle area (max 6). Only Units attack. A Unit cannot attack on the turn it was
  deployed unless it is a Link Unit.
- Pilot: paired under one of your Units (one Pilot per Unit). It adds its AP/HP and gives the Unit the
  text printed below the Pilot's name. If the Pilot matches the Unit's link condition, the Unit
  becomes a Link Unit: it can attack right away and turns on 【When Linked】 and 【During Link】 text.
  If the Unit leaves play, its Pilot goes with it.
- Command: a one-off effect, then it goes to the trash. 【Main】 commands are played in your main
  phase; 【Action】 commands can also be played during action steps, including on the opponent's
  turn. A command with 【Pilot】 can instead be paired as a Pilot.
- Base: goes in your base section (one at a time) and soaks up attacks before your Shields.
- Resource: pays costs. Lives in the resource deck, not the main deck.

## Attacking
Rest an active Unit to attack either the opponent or one of their rested Units. You cannot normally
attack an active Unit.
1. Attack: triggers 【Attack】 effects.
2. Block: the defender may rest one active Unit with <Blocker> to make it the new target.
3. Action step: both players may play 【Action】 cards and effects.
4. Damage:
   - Unit vs Unit: both deal damage equal to their AP at the same time. A Unit whose damage reaches
     its HP is destroyed. Damage stays on a Unit until something repairs it.
   - Attacking the opponent: the damage goes to their Base if they have one. Otherwise their top
     Shield takes it, and any damage (1 or more) destroys that one Shield; extra damage is lost. If
     they have no Base and no Shields, the attack wins the game.

A destroyed Shield is revealed. If it has 【Burst】, its owner may use that effect for free. Shields do
not go to hand unless an effect says so.

Attacking rests your Unit, and it stays rested through the opponent's next turn. A rested Unit
cannot block and can be attacked. Every attack is also a decision about defence.

## Keywords
- <Repair X>: at the end of your turn, this Unit recovers X HP.
- <Breach X>: when this Unit destroys an enemy Unit in battle on your turn, deal X damage to the
  enemy's Base, or their top Shield if they have no Base.
- <Support X>: rest this Unit to give another friendly Unit +X AP this turn.
- <Blocker>: can be rested to redirect an enemy attack onto itself.
- <First Strike>: when attacking, deals its damage first; if that destroys the target, it takes none back.
- <High-Maneuver>: while attacking, enemy Units cannot use <Blocker>.
- <Suppression>: when it hits Shields, it damages the top two Shields at once.
- 【Deploy】 when played, 【Attack】 when it attacks, 【Destroyed】 when destroyed, 【When Paired】 when a
  Pilot is paired, 【Once per Turn】 limits an effect to once each turn.

## Fundamentals
- Shields are your life total, and the Base is a buffer in front of them. Removing a Base usually
  takes one big hit or two small ones.
- Tempo: using your full Lv. of resources every turn matters most in the early game.
- Board: an active Unit threatens to block; a rested Unit is exposed. Count what the opponent can
  swing back with before sending everything.
- Card advantage: trading one card for two of theirs wins long games. Aggro decks give it up to win
  first.
- Burst: every Shield you break might trigger a free effect for the opponent. Breaking Shields is
  still how you win, but factor in that the opponent's hand grows stronger as they lose Shields.
