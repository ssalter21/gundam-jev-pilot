# Aggro

Aggro wins by breaking Shields before the opponent's stronger late game comes online. It plays a
low curve of cheap Units, attacks early and often, and uses its cards to keep the path to the
opponent clear rather than to win long fights.

## How to think
- Damage to the opponent is the goal; the board is only a means. A Unit that hits a Shield every
  turn is worth more than one that trades evenly.
- Spend all your resources every turn in the early game. An unused resource on turns 1-4 is lost damage.
- Remove Blockers and the Base; ignore big Units that are not in the way unless they will kill you first.
- Link Units matter: they attack the turn they arrive, which is often the surprise damage that closes games.
- Count the race. If you win the race, keep attacking and do not block. If you lose it, block
  just enough to survive one more turn of attacks.
- Card advantage does not matter much. Trading a card for a Shield is usually good.
- Aggro runs out of cards. When your hand is empty and the opponent has stabilised, every attack
  must still count: pick the ones that break Shields or remove Blockers.

## Common mistakes
- Holding Units back "to be safe" when the opponent has no board.
- Attacking into a Unit and losing yours when it didn't remove a Blocker or open lethal.
- Not reading the opponent's active Blockers before committing the whole board.

## Game plans

### fast_start
When: turns 1-3, or while we have fewer than 3 Units on board. Do: deploy the cheapest Units first
to use every resource. Attack the Base or Shields with any Unit that can, unless the opponent has a
Unit that would kill ours for free on the swing back. Do not trade Units except to remove a Blocker.

### keep_pushing
When: we have more Units or more total AP than the opponent. Do: attack the opponent every turn with
all Units that survive the crack-back. Pair Pilots onto the Units that attack, preferring links so
new Units can attack immediately. Use removal only on Blockers or on a Unit about to trade with ours.

### close_the_game
When: the opponent has 2 or fewer Shields and no Base, or this turn's attacks can remove every
Shield and then hit them. Do: send everything at the opponent. Remove or bypass Blockers first. Ignore
trades and card advantage. Keep nothing back unless we lose on their next turn.

### race_or_stabilise
When: the opponent has more total AP on board than we do and we have 3 or fewer Shields. Do: if
we still win the race (our damage per turn is enough to finish first), keep attacking. Otherwise keep
our Blockers active, trade only when our Unit survives or theirs is bigger, and set up one big
turn of attacks instead of chipping.
