# Midrange

Midrange wins by having the better Units on the board in the middle turns. It plays efficient
Units that are hard to kill, trades well, and turns board control into damage once the opponent
can no longer keep up. It can be the aggressor against control and the defender against aggro.

## How to think
- Ask "who is the beatdown?" every turn. Against faster decks, protect your Shields and win the
  board first. Against slower decks, apply pressure before their late game comes online.
- Good trades are how midrange gets ahead: attack rested enemy Units your Units kill and survive.
- Pilots and link effects turn average Units into the best ones on the table. Pair when it wins a
  fight or makes a Unit hard to remove, not just because it is possible.
- Keep one or two active Units as defence while attacking with the rest. A board that attacks with
  everything cannot block.
- Removal is best spent on the opponent's best Unit or their key Link Unit, not on small Units.
- When you are clearly ahead on board, turn it into Shield damage steadily; do not give them free
  trades.

## Common mistakes
- Racing an aggro deck instead of taking the board.
- Sitting back against control until their late-game Units arrive.
- Spending removal early on cheap Units and having none for the real threat.

## Game plans

### develop
When: turns 1-3, or while both boards are small. Do: deploy the best Unit we can afford each turn,
using all resources. Attack the Base or Shields only with Units that stay safe on the swing back.
Trade with enemy Units when ours survives.

### take_the_board
When: the opponent has Units on board that threaten us, or roughly equal boards. Do: attack rested
enemy Units that we destroy and survive. Pair Pilots to win fights. Use removal on their strongest
Unit or Link Unit. Keep at least one active Blocker or strong Unit back.

### convert_advantage
When: we have more Units or clearly more total AP than the opponent. Do: attack Shields and the
Base with Units that are not needed for defence, every turn. Keep trading off anything they deploy.
Hold one answer for their next big threat if we can.

### close_the_game
When: the opponent has 2 or fewer Shields and no Base, or this turn's attacks can remove every
Shield and then hit them. Do: send everything needed at the opponent, remove Blockers first,
ignore trades.

### defend
When: the opponent has more total AP than we have and we have 3 or fewer Shields. Do: stop
attacking with Units we need as blockers, deploy Blockers and Bases, remove their biggest attacker,
and only attack when it is safe or a trade in our favour.
