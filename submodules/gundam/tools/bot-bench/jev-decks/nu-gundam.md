## Overview
Low-curve aggro. Take enough early shields or extremely card efficient trades so that a well set up double breach from Nu Gundam Lv7 pair can finish the game.

## Key plays
- Level <4: Get many units onto the board to take shields or set up explosive turns of multiple shields destroyed.
- Level 4+: Amuro link with Gundam ST01-001 to rest a unit, and either kill it if you survive and then repair away most of the damage, or kill a base to setup a big run on the shields from your other units.
- Level 7+: pair Amuro with GD05-017 to trigger a potential 2 combats in 1 turn, each breaching for 5. Using GD05-125 helps it survive combats it otherwise wouldnt.

## Game plans

### early_pressure
When: turns 1-4, or whenever the opponent has fewer units than us.
Do: deploy a unit every turn, prioritising the cheapest units first so no resources are wasted. Attack shields directly with every unit that would survive the crack-back, and do not trade units unless the trade removes a Blocker.

### close_the_game
When: the opponent has 2 or fewer shields and no base, or any time a set of attacks this turn can remove all shields and then hit them.
Do: send everything at the opponent directly. Use Hawk of Endymion on their Blocker first. Ignore trades and card advantage.

## Rules
- Always take an attack option that says it wins the game.
- If a direct attack on me would lose me the game, block it if any option allows.
- Deploy cheap units before pilots and commands on turns 1-3.
- Do not attack into an enemy unit if the option says mine is destroyed and theirs survives.
