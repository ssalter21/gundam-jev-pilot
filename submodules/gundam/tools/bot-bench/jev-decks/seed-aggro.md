# SEED Aggro (ST04 example)

## Decklist
4 ST04-004   # Moebius
4 ST04-008   # Ginn
4 ST04-002   # Strike Gundam
4 ST04-003   # Moebius Zero
4 ST04-005   # Strike Dagger
4 ST04-009   # Miguel's Ginn
4 ST04-007   # Aegis Gundam (MA Mode)
4 ST04-010   # Kira Yamato
4 ST04-011   # Athrun Zala
4 ST04-012   # Striker Pack
4 ST04-013   # Hawk of Endymion
4 ST04-014   # The Magic Bullet of Dusk
2 ST04-015   # Archangel
resource 10 R-001

## Overview
Low-curve aggro. Flood the board with cheap units on turns 1-3, pair Kira or Athrun onto
Strike Gundam or Aegis from level 4, and push shields fast before the opponent's bigger
units take over. Tempo commands (Hawk of Endymion bounce, Magic Bullet first strike) clear
the one blocker that stands between us and a lethal swing.

## Game plans

### early_pressure
When: turns 1-4, or whenever the opponent has fewer units than us. Do: deploy a unit every
turn, prioritising the cheapest units first so no resources are wasted. Attack shields directly
with every unit that would survive the crack-back, and do not trade units unless the trade
removes a Blocker.

### build_link_threat
When: we reach level 4 and have Strike Gundam or Aegis on the board with Kira or Athrun in hand.
Do: pair the pilot before attacking. Prefer Kira on the unit that will attack into a big enemy,
Athrun when we need to hit an active enemy unit. Keep one cheap unit back if the opponent has more
total AP than we have shields.

### close_the_game
When: the opponent has 2 or fewer shields and no base, or any time a set of attacks this turn can
remove all shields and then hit them. Do: send everything at the opponent directly. Use Hawk of
Endymion on their Blocker first. Ignore trades and card advantage.

### stabilise
When: the opponent has more total AP on board than we do and we have 2 or fewer shields.
Do: stop attacking with units we need as blockers, deploy Moebius and Archangel, use Hawk of
Endymion on their biggest attacker, and trade units only when ours survives or theirs is bigger.

## Rules
- Always take an attack option that says it wins the game.
- If a direct attack on me would lose me the game, block it if any option allows.
- Deploy cheap units before pilots and commands on turns 1-3.
- Do not attack into an enemy unit if the option says mine is destroyed and theirs survives.
- Deploy Archangel when we have 3 or fewer shields and no base; its Deploy effect puts one of our shields into our hand.
- Keep 1 resource open for Hawk of Endymion when the opponent has a big Blocker and we are closing.

## Card notes
- ST04-004: Moebius is a 1-cost Blocker. Good early, better kept back in the stabilise plan.
- ST04-002: Strike Gundam draws then discards when deployed; discard duplicate commands or a spare base.
- ST04-007: Aegis has Breach 3, so killing a unit with it also breaks a shield. Aim it at enemy units it can kill.
- ST04-009: Miguel's Ginn is 3 AP but only 1 HP; attack with it, never block with it.
- ST04-010: Kira Yamato makes the enemy unit in battle lose 2 AP, so a paired unit wins fights it otherwise loses.
- ST04-011: Athrun Zala lets the paired unit attack an active enemy unit (Lv5 or lower) this turn.
- ST04-012: Striker Pack makes a Strike token with Blocker; take Sword (AP 4) when attacking, Launcher (HP 4) when defending.
- ST04-013: Hawk of Endymion returns an enemy unit with 3 or less HP to hand. Best on a Blocker right before a lethal swing.
- ST04-014: Magic Bullet of Dusk gives a Lv2-or-lower unit First Strike this turn, so it can kill something bigger and survive.
- ST04-015: Archangel is a base that also returns a shield to hand when deployed.
